1.3 Information Collected When Using Online Features

Certain features require an internet connection and temporarily process your data. These features are clearly marked with an "online" badge in the App:

  • Receipt Scanning: Your receipt photo is sent to Google Cloud Vision API for text extraction, then to Google Gemini AI for expense categorization. Images are processed in real-time and are not stored on third-party servers.
  • AI Coach: Your transaction summary and monthly financial data are sent to Google Gemini AI to generate financial coaching advice. Only aggregated summary data is sent, not individual transaction details.
  • Palm Reading (Palad Guide): Your palm photo is sent to Google Gemini AI for entertainment-only palm reading. Images are processed in real-time and not stored.
  • Daily Fortune (Daily Swerte): Your profile information (birthday, gender, zodiac sign, birth hour, region) is sent to generate your daily fortune.
  • AI Chat (Kuwentuhan with AI): When you send a message in the AI Chat screen, the recent conversation window (up to the last 12 messages, with each user message capped at 4,000 characters) and the language setting you selected in the App are sent to Google Gemini AI to generate a reply. The last user message is additionally converted into a 768-dimension embedding by Google's embedding model and used to look up similar memory entries previously stored for your account (see Section 1.2, "AI Chat memories"). After the reply is returned, a small extraction call to Google Gemini AI inspects only the most recent user message and the assistant's reply to identify new facts the user explicitly stated about themselves; up to five short facts (≤200 characters each) may then be stored on our servers as AI Chat memories. The full chat transcript is not stored on our servers — only the daily token-usage counters described in Section 1.2 and the extracted facts. AI Chat is restricted to identified accounts (anonymous sessions are not allowed) and is subject to a daily token quota and an hourly request rate limit. The full chat history is kept only on your device (see Section 1.1 and "Local AI Chat history" below).
  • Paluwagan Payment Receipts: When you tap "Analyze screenshot" on the Paluwagan payment screen, the receipt screenshot you uploaded is sent to Google Gemini AI to suggest the payment amount, reference number, and platform (e.g., GCash/Maya). The image is processed in real-time on Google's servers solely for this purpose and is not stored by Google. The original screenshot remains stored in our servers for the group's audit record (see Section 1.2). Your separate explicit consent is requested the first time you use any AI feature, and you may decline; declining only disables the auto-suggest and you can still enter the values manually.
  • Phone Verification (SMS OTP): Phone verification is required to use the Paluwagan feature, and is also used to transfer your account to a new device. When you start the verification flow, your phone number is sent to Supabase Auth, which delivers a one-time passcode to your device through an SMS messaging provider integrated with Supabase Auth. After successful verification, your phone number is stored in our authentication system, and a SHA-256 hash of your phone number (combined with a server-side pepper) is used inside Paluwagan groups so that other members can confirm your participation without seeing your raw number.

1.5 Cross-Group Defaulter History (Paluwagan only)

If you participate in the Paluwagan feature and you are confirmed as a defaulter by your group's vote, the App may, upon another Paluwagan group leader attempting to invite you to a different group, present that leader with a summary consisting of:

  • the count of your confirmed defaults across groups,
  • the count of post-default repayments (records marked as defaulter_paid), and
  • the most recent confirmation date.

The summary is keyed by your scrambled (SHA-256-hashed) phone number and never includes the group name, member names, monetary amounts, or any other identifying detail. This disclosure is internal to the App between users acting in the role of group leaders and is not shared with any third party.

Your explicit, informed consent for this cross-group disclosure is obtained at the time you sign each Paluwagan group's agreement (consent item 7). The disclosure consent is a precondition to participating in any Paluwagan group: members who do not give this consent cannot join Paluwagan groups. Once given, the consent remains in effect for the entire duration of every Paluwagan group you participate in. You may withdraw the consent only when you have no active Paluwagan memberships, and any defaulter records confirmed before withdrawal will remain shared in line with the consent given at the time of the confirmation. Withdrawal stops future cross-group disclosures but does not retroactively invalidate processing that already occurred.

Withholding consent does not exempt you from a defaulter vote within an existing group; it only restricts cross-group disclosure. Defaulter records are processed by us as a personal information controller under Section 12(a) and (f) of the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (consent, and the legitimate interest of other group leaders in protecting against repeat default), and your rights as a data subject under Section 16 of the Act remain available, subject to the contractual and legitimate interest constraints described above.

6. Data Security

We implement the following security measures to protect your data:

  • Sensitive data (auth tokens, profile) is stored inside your device's secure storage (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android). Your app lock PIN is stored only as a one-way SHA-256 hash — the PIN itself is never written to disk or sent to our servers.
  • Screen capture prevention is enabled to protect your financial data
  • All network communications use HTTPS encryption
  • Server-side data is protected by Row Level Security (RLS) policies — only you can access your own data
  • End-to-end encrypted backups: Backup files are compressed and then encrypted on your device using AES-256-GCM with a key derived from your passphrase (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256, 200,000 iterations) before they are uploaded. The passphrase and the derived key never leave your device, and we have no technical ability to decrypt your backup. If you lose your passphrase, your backup cannot be recovered — there is no recovery key and no server-side escrow. Encrypted backups are stored with access-controlled signed URLs and automatically deleted from our servers 30 days after upload.
  • App lock with PIN and optional biometric authentication
