What's Inside

Offline core, online when you want it 🧩

Your money data lives on your phone. Online features only send data when you tap them — and each one wears an online badge so there are no surprises.

💰

Everyday Money

📵 Works offline
  • 💵 Income, expenses & one-tap Quick Expense
  • 📦 Envelope budgeting per period
  • 🤝 Utang tracker with Hulugan payment schedule
  • 🐷 Dream Board savings goals
  • 📅 Calendar view of every transaction
  • 💡 Utility Bill Calculator & Estimator
  • 🏠 Home screen widgets

No account, no internet needed. Works the moment you install.

🤖

AI Helpers

🌐 Online · opt-in
  • 🧾 AI receipt scanning — instant entry from a photo
  • 📊 AI Coach — gentle, personalized spending insights
  • 💬 Kuwentuhan with AI — chat about your money
  • 🔮 Daily Swerte & Palad Guide — just for fun

You choose when to use them. Nothing is sent until you tap an online feature.

👛

Save Together

🌐 Online · phone-verified
  • 🔄 Paluwagan — run rotating savings with your barkada
  • 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Shared Wallet — one ledger for family & OFW funds
  • ➕ Track contributions & expenses together
  • 🔐 Phone-verified members, hashed numbers only

Built for trust: a record-keeping tool between people you know — never a lender or fund-holder.

🔒

Private & Secure

🛡️ By design
  • 📱 Your money data stays on your device by default
  • 🔑 End-to-end encrypted backups — even we can't read them
  • 🚫 No ads, no trackers, no selling your data
  • 🔓 App lock with PIN & biometrics

Made to comply with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012.
